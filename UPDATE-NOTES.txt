ORVION v16 — REFINEMENT PASS (on top of v15)

CHANGED BASED ON FEEDBACK
- The green completion bar under the poster is gone. A finished title (all episodes marked, or status Watched) shows no bar at all;
  only in-progress shows keep a thin accent bar. Same in Continue watching.

NEW
- Your rating: tap 1–5 stars in the Manage sheet (tap the same star again to clear). Saved instantly as one optional field
  ("rating") on the entry. Shown on the card as "★ 4", sortable ("Your rating"), and summarised in Statistics (average + distribution).
- Surprise me: on Plan to watch, picks a random title (respects the category chip and filter) and opens its details.
- Episode tracker: opens scrolled to your next episode; marking an episode ahead of unwatched ones offers "Mark them" in one tap;
  light haptic feedback on phones that support it (off when animations are None).
- Settings > Data > Export backup (JSON): read-only download of your whole library (titles, statuses, ratings, progress). No writes.
- Duplicate notice is now quiet: shown only on tabs that actually contain a duplicated title.
- Smoother scrolling: off-screen sections/cards skip rendering until needed; press feedback on Continue watching cards.

DATA: only the optional field "rating" is new (written when you tap a star). Existing data/IDs/schema untouched.

FILES CHANGED IN v16: css/style.css, js/ui.js, js/app.js, js/tracker.js, js/stats.js, js/appearance.js, js/watchlist.js (adds saveRating only), watchlist.html

ORVION v15 — CINEMA REDESIGN (visual + interaction redesign; data layer untouched)

DATA SAFETY
- NOT touched in this release: js/watchlist.js, js/api.js, js/discover.js, js/search.js, manifest.json, vercel.json, icons/*.
  Firebase config, collection, document IDs, schema, TMDB integration, saved progress and statistics maths are byte-identical to v14.
- Only localStorage keys added: "orvion-nav" (navigation style). Existing keys (orvion-theme, orvion-color, ...) are still honoured.
- Reminder from v14: Orvion has no Firebase Auth; the shared "watchlist" collection is protected only by your Firestore rules. Review them.

NEW VISUAL SYSTEM
- Apple-style type (system font stack, no web fonts), charcoal / true-AMOLED surfaces, light "grouped" theme.
- Restrained accent: used for progress, selection and the active tab only. Primary buttons are inverted (white on dark, black on light).
- Clean posters: nothing sits on the artwork. Title, rating and one intentional control ("+" / status / "...") live below it;
  no hover-only actions, no ticks or badges on posters; 2:3 posters, 16:9 backdrop cards for Trending.

SCREENS (all re-composed, not re-coloured)
- Home: full-bleed cinematic hero (cross-fade, Ken-Burns drift, staggered title/meta/buttons, timed segment pager,
  swipe, arrow keys, desktop arrows, pause on hover/focus/touch, off for reduced motion) -> Continue watching (list cards with
  an explicit check button) -> Your watchlist -> Trending (backdrop cards) -> Popular -> Top rated -> Popular series.
- Discover / Search / Watchlist: large titles, iOS segmented controls, chip rows, custom dropdown menus (replace native selects),
  poster grids, batched rendering, title filter.
- Details: cinematic backdrop sheet, one primary action, Episodes shortcut, trailer, facts list, cast.
- Episode tracker: progress ring + Watched/Left/Season figures, "Up next" card with a one-tap button, season tiles with mini
  progress, one grouped episode list with big check targets, Up-next marker, locked unaired episodes, Mark all/Clear, Undo, long-press "up to here".
- Statistics: same real data, new layout and typography (grouped panels, big numerals).
- Settings (gear icon): Appearance > Navigation style (live tappable preview), Theme (mode incl. true Black, presets, accent + custom),
  Display (glass, density, poster corners, animations).

NAVIGATION STYLES (Settings > Appearance > Navigation style) — same links/routing, four looks, animated switch, persisted
  Native (full-width tab bar, top indicator) · Liquid Glass (floating, glass lens indicator) ·
  Minimal (compact, icons only, dot indicator) · Cinematic (wide glass, active label rises, tinted pill).
  The container, indicator, labels and spacing morph with CSS transitions; the indicator is re-measured every frame during the morph.

MOTION
- Page content rises in / fades out between screens (dock and top bar stay), sheets use an iOS-style curve and swipe-down,
  press feedback on buttons, drawn check marks, growing progress bars, toasts, menus. Settings > Animations: Auto / Full / Subtle / None;
  Auto follows the device's reduced-motion setting.
